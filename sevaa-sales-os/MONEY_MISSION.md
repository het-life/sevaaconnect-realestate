# MONEY MISSION

## Objective
Build a lawful, auditable system that can reach **₹1,00,000/month of sustainable owner-withdrawable cash** while continuing to reinvest for growth.

Starting capital envelope: **₹50,000–₹1,00,000**.

This is a target, not a guaranteed return. Producing ₹1 lakh every month from only ₹1 lakh of financial-market capital would imply roughly 100% monthly returns before costs and tax, so trading is not the base-case plan. The primary engine is **customer-funded recurring business revenue**.

## Mission hierarchy
1. Managed SEVAA Sales OS: acquire and retain paying B2B customers in construction/prefab/interiors/contracting.
2. Productize into SaaS: reduce marginal service effort until one operator can support many customers.
3. Experimental capital loops: paper-only until evidence is strong.

## Current planning economics
- Managed subscription: ₹14,999/month + GST.
- Setup fee: ₹14,999 + GST.
- Gateway planning cost: 2.36% of collected invoice value, conservatively approximating a 2% platform fee plus 18% GST on that fee.
- GST: 18% pass-through planning assumption for the service; actual classification must be confirmed.
- Company-tax reserve: 25.168% planning assumption if section 115BAA applies (22% tax + 10% surcharge + 4% cess).
- Fixed operating cost: ₹4,500/month.
- Variable automation/support cost: ₹700/customer/month.
- Post-tax reinvestment reserve: 20%.

At this modeled cost structure, approximately **13 active customers** are required to leave roughly ₹1 lakh/month withdrawable while still retaining the 20% reinvestment reserve. Recalculate; do not treat 13 as immutable truth.

## Paper → real promotion rule
Do not turn simulated acquisition spend into real spend merely because a simulation looks profitable. Promotion requires:
- at least one real external enquiry through the system;
- at least one paid pilot;
- measured conversion data in the ledger;
- explicit founder-approved acquisition budget;
- automated stop rules so cash reserve and CAC/payback limits cannot be breached.

## Accounting discipline
GST is not operating revenue. Payment fees, infrastructure/API/support cost, refunds/bad debt when observed, tax reserve and reinvestment must be deducted before reporting owner-withdrawable cash. Replace modeled values with actual ledger values one assumption at a time.
