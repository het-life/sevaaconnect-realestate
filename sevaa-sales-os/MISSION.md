# SEVAA SALES OS — MISSION

## North star
Build a low-touch, lawful money engine that converts a small capital base into recurring customer-funded revenue, with a target of **₹1,00,000/month owner-withdrawable cash while still reinvesting for growth**.

`MONEY_MISSION.md` is the economic source of truth. Paper models must remain clearly separated from real ledger data.

## Strategy order
1. Prove the managed SEVAA Sales OS can acquire and retain paying construction/prefab/interior/contracting businesses.
2. Automate onboarding, lead handling, proposal creation, follow-up, payment reconciliation and reporting so founder work becomes exception-only.
3. Reinvest measured operating profit into the best observed acquisition channel.
4. Productize into a multi-tenant SaaS only after paid demand is proven.
5. Keep financial-market/trading experiments paper-only and outside the primary revenue plan.

## Revenue loop
Traffic / referral / lawful outreach → public quote page → normalized lead → score/qualification → proposal draft → founder approval → secure buyer link → payment link → signed payment verification → won-value ledger → retention / referral → measured reinvestment.

## Success gates
software verified → public deployment → first external enquiry → first approved buyer proposal → first verified paid pilot → repeatable CAC/payback measurement → 5 active customers → 10 active customers → modeled ₹1L/month withdrawal capacity using observed data → actual ₹1L/month withdrawal capacity for three trailing months after operating costs and tax reserve.

## Safety / trust boundaries
- No secrets in Git/logs.
- No autonomous borrowing, leverage, securities/crypto trading, bank transfers, payouts or refunds.
- No autonomous price override or contractual commitment.
- No unsolicited bulk messaging.
- Founder approval remains required for proposal approval and real payment-link creation.
- Real acquisition spend remains disabled until a paid pilot and an explicit founder budget cap exist.

## Efficiency rule
Future agents start with the machine-readable state/checkpoint, read only files required for the current action, and turn repeated operations into scripts/tests/endpoints/modules.
